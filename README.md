Leonida Ledger is an offline GTA VI trophy guide and tracker. It covers all 55 PS5 trophies with cited, own-words guides, and ships a checklist tracker that saves your progress in localStorage with export/import, so you can tick trophies off as you play without ever losing your place. Filters let you narrow by trophy type, difficulty, and missable status, a spoiler setting keeps story details hidden until you want them, and a 3-phase roadmap lays out the fastest route to the platinum — with missable trophies flagged up front so nothing gets locked out of reach.

## Run it

```sh
npx serve app
# or
python3 -m http.server --directory app
```
